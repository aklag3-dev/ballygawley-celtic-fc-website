About Our Club

A community built around the beautiful game

Founded in 1980, Ballygawley Celtic FC has been the heartbeat of football in Ballygawley, Co. Sligo for over four decades. As a community-run club, we are driven by volunteers, parents, and passionate football people who believe in the power of the game to bring people together.

We field teams across multiple age groups, from underage boys and girls to our senior men's team, competing in the Sligo Leitrim & District Soccer League. Our home ground is the Ballygawley Community Park, where players train and compete in the spirit of fair play and development.

We are proud to hold FAI Club Mark accreditation, recognising our commitment to best practice in governance, coaching, child safeguarding, and community engagement.

Getting Here

  • By car: Ballygawley is approx. 10km from Sligo town on the N16/N15
  • By bus: Bus 566 (Sligo–Dowra) and Bus 572 (Sligo–Ballinamore) via TFI Local Link
  • Parking: Available at Ballygawley Community Park
